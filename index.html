rules

1Every card must be read aloud so everyone knows what task you have been given.
2Any card that refers to the Taskmaster is referring to Anna.
3If you get a card that has an effect that lasts the whole game and you fail the condition of the card, you drink and you no longer have to follow the rules of that card.
4If you get more than one card which has an effect that lasts for the whole game, the new card will override the first card and you only have to do what the new card says.
5Least amount of drinks wins! The Taskmaster will give the winner a prize so it is worth trying to not lose, even if you aren’t drinking alcohol!
6The person with the most drinks will suffer a punishment determined by the group. After all, drunkenness and gluttony is a sin!
